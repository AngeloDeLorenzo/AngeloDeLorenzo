# Working method

I start from the problem and context of use, then organise content, data,
interactions and system responsibilities. WordPress may be an editorial site,
an application backend or an e-commerce foundation: the choice follows the work
the system must support.

Accessibility, performance, SEO and measurement belong in the development
cycle. Documentation, checks and human review also make AI-assisted work more
reliable.

The goal is a platform that people can understand, use and maintain, not simply
a published page.
