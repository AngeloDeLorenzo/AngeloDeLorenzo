# Profile and contribution

Angelo De Lorenzo builds WordPress websites, plugins, e-commerce platforms, integrations and operational tools. His work includes web quality, accessibility, content and working systems for applied AI.

For direct clients he also handles requirements, proposals and estimates, priorities, deadlines and communication. He has worked with WordPress since 2012, connecting implementation with collaboration with people and teams.

Since 2018 he has worked at CGM Consulting as Senior Web Solutions & Digital Systems Specialist. At CGM Verse, since 2023, his public role is Creative Director. These experiences demonstrate both hands-on work and collaboration with technical and creative contributors.
